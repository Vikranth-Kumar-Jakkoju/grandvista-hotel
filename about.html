Nearly A Century of Distinction
Our Story

A Living Landmark of Indian Grace & Colonial Charm

Founded as a distinguished consular estate in 1928, GrandVista was envisioned as an architectural oasis where timeless hospitality meets understated grandeur.

Over nearly a century, our corridors have welcomed heads of state, renowned literary figures, and royalty. Each space has been preserved with meticulous craftsmanship—walnut paneling, Italian travertine, and hand-carved stone arches—while seamlessly evolving with modern, sustainable hospitality technology.

Our Enduring Mission

To provide discerning global travelers an authentic haven of peace, privacy, and warm Indian hospitality. We believe luxury is found not in extravagance, but in effortless service, exquisite craftsmanship, and anticipatory care.

The GrandVista Philosophy

Rooted in the Sanskrit ethos of "Atithi Devo Bhava" (the guest is divine), we treat every arrival as a cherished occasion. Whether it is an evening glass of vintage port in our cellar or a customized pillow menu, your comfort is our sole measure of success.
