Terms & Conditions of Service

Reservation agreements, check-in guidelines, cancellation terms, and guest conduct standards.

📌 Educational Demonstration Notice: This website is an educational software development project representing a simulated 5-star hotel brand. No actual room reservations or financial liabilities are created by submitting forms on this platform.

1. Reservation Agreements & Confirmation

By initiating a reservation through the GrandVista online booking stepper, guests submit a formal reservation request. Each request generates a unique reference code (e.g. GVH-2026-#####) assigned an initial status of Pending Confirmation until formally verified by our front desk.

2. Payment on Arrival & Tax Compliance

All direct website bookings operate on a Payment on Arrival model. No advance credit card prepayment is required during online booking. Full accommodation charges, selected add-on services, and applicable statutory taxes (18% Goods & Services Tax) are settled upon check-in at the front desk.

3. Cancellation & Modification Timeline

GrandVista provides flexible cancellation terms for direct website guests:

  • Complimentary Cancellation: Permitted up to forty-eight (48) hours prior to the scheduled check-in time (2:00 PM local time).
  • Late Cancellations & No-Shows: Cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival may be subject to a charge equivalent to the first night's room rate plus taxes.

4. Check-In & Check-Out Protocols

  • Standard Check-In: Commences daily from 2:00 PM local time. Government-issued photo identification (Passport, Driving License, or National ID) is required for all adult guests.
  • Standard Check-Out: Concludes by 12:00 PM (Noon). Late check-outs up to 4:00 PM may be pre-arranged via our add-on packages or subject to room availability.

5. Guest Conduct & Hotel Property Rules

GrandVista Hotel maintains a serene, smoke-free residential atmosphere. Smoking is strictly prohibited within all interior guest suites, restaurants, and spa facilities, with designated open-air garden lounges provided. Damage to hotel furnishings or bespoke artwork will be evaluated and charged to the guest's folio upon departure.
