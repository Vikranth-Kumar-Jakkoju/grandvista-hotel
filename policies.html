Guest Policies & Stay Guidelines

Please review our guest policies to ensure a seamless, tranquil, and comfortable luxury stay at GrandVista.

ℹ️ Notice: These terms represent the official demo operating policies for the GrandVista Hotel student project.

Check-In Policy

Official check-in begins at 2:00 PM local Indian Standard Time. Early check-in requests are subject to room availability upon arrival unless reserved as a guaranteed add-on during booking. Guests arriving prior to 2:00 PM are welcome to store luggage with the concierge and enjoy public dining facilities.

Check-Out Policy

Check-out time is scheduled by 12:00 PM (Noon). Late check-out until 4:00 PM is subject to availability and incurs a flat fee of ₹1,000. Departures after 6:00 PM are billed at the prevailing full nightly room rate.

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Reservations may be cancelled without penalty up to 48 hours prior to the arrival date (2:00 PM local hotel time). Cancellations received within 48 hours or non-arrivals (no-shows) will be subject to a fee equal to one night's room charge plus applicable taxes.

Children & Extra Bedding

Children under 6 years of age stay complimentary when sharing existing bedding with parents. Baby cribs for infants (0–2 years) are available complimentary upon advance request. Extra rollaway beds for older children or third adults are available for ₹800 per night.

Pet Policy

Service animals assisting guests with disabilities are warmly welcomed throughout all public and guest room areas without fee. Certified small domestic pets (under 10 kg) are permitted exclusively in select ground-floor garden suites upon prior arrangement.

Government Identification & Age

In compliance with regulatory guidelines, all adult guests must present valid government-issued photographic identification upon check-in (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, or Driver's License for Indian nationals; valid Passport & Visa for foreign nationals). PAN cards are not accepted as address proof. The primary registering guest must be at least 18 years of age.

Non-Smoking Environment

GrandVista is a 100% smoke-free hospitality environment. Smoking and electronic vaping are strictly prohibited inside all guest bedrooms, suites, hallways, and indoor public spaces. A deep-cleaning recovery fee of ₹5,000 will be assessed for smoking violations. Designated smoking areas are provided in the outdoor garden courtyards.

Payment Methods & Currency

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Diners Club), direct UPI bank transfers, and cash in Indian National Rupees (INR). A refundable incidental hold or credit card pre-authorization is collected upon arrival and released upon departure inspection.
