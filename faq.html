Guest Inquiries & Answers

Everything you need to know about your arrival, suite amenities, flexible cancellation, and bespoke experiences.

Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM, and check-out is scheduled until 12:00 PM (Noon). If you arrive early, our concierge will gladly secure your luggage while you relax at the rooftop pool or enjoy fine dining.

Yes. GrandVista offers private chauffeured luxury sedan transfers to and from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) for a flat fee of ₹1,200 per journey. You can select this add-on directly during Step 4 of the online reservation flow or notify our concierge 24 hours prior to arrival.

Select specialty suites (Executive Suite and Grand Suite) include complimentary daily breakfast for all staying guests. For standard reservations, our gourmet multi-cuisine breakfast buffet can be added at booking for ₹600 per night.

We provide 100% complimentary, secure subterranean valet parking for all staying guests and restaurant patrons. Fast EV charging stations (CCS2 and Type-2) are also available in our basement garage.

Yes. Enterprise-grade optical fiber Wi-Fi 6 is provided with our compliments in all suites, restaurants, executive meeting salons, and poolside cabanas with speeds up to 300 Mbps.

Infant cribs (up to 2 years) are provided complimentary upon request. Rollaway luxury plush extra beds for adults or older children can be arranged in spacious rooms and suites for ₹800 per night.

We offer a flexible demo cancellation policy. You may cancel or modify your reservation without penalty up to 48 hours prior to your arrival date (2:00 PM local hotel time). Cancellations within 48 hours incur a one-night room charge.

Early check-in from 11:00 AM is complimentary based on room availability upon arrival. Guaranteed late check-out until 4:00 PM can be reserved during online booking for a flat fee of ₹1,000.

GrandVista houses the Imperial Ballroom (accommodating up to 500 guests), two private boardroom suites, and outdoor garden terraces. We provide complete audiovisual support, bespoke catering menus, and dedicated event coordinators.

All resident guests enjoy complimentary, unlimited access to both the rooftop infinity pool (6:00 AM – 10:00 PM) and the 24/7 TechnoGym fitness centre.

Still have an unanswered question? Our concierge team is available 24/7.
