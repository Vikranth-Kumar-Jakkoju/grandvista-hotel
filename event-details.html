The Grand Ballroom

Request Event Proposal

An architectural masterpiece combining timeless imperial grandeur with 21st-century event technology. Featuring 8,000 square feet of completely pillarless open expanse, soaring 22-foot acoustic ceilings, motorized stage trusses, and dedicated pre-function foyers, The Grand Ballroom caters to state galas, global conventions, and bespoke weddings of up to 500 attendees.

Seating & Capacity Specifications

Theatre Layout 500 Attendees • Clear sightlines • Central aisle
Classroom Setup 300 Delegates • Powered executive desks
Banquet Rounds 380 Guests • 10-top round tables with dance floor
Cocktail Reception 550 Guests • High cocktail tables & open bars

Technical Infrastructure & Services

Audiovisual & Lighting

Dual 4K laser projection, Shure wireless line mics, multi-zone Bose acoustic speakers, motorized rigging trusses.

Dedicated Green Rooms

Private bridal and VIP suites with direct backstage access, en-suite dressing amenities, and private security controls.

Artisanal Culinary Service

Custom menus curated by Executive Master Chefs, live culinary theater stations, dietary-certified options, sommelier wine pairings.
