Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Our commitment to guest confidentiality, transparency, and international data privacy standards.

📌 Demonstration Website Notice: This website is an educational demonstration project. No real financial transactions are executed, and credit cards are not processed online.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with GrandVista Hotel—whether exploring accommodations, submitting reservation requests, subscribing to culinary updates, or contacting our concierge desk—we collect information necessary to deliver bespoke hospitality experiences:

  • Contact Details: Full name, verified email address, contact telephone number, and residential nationality.
  • Reservation Specifics: Check-in and check-out dates, selected room category, guest count, and personalized stay preferences (e.g. dietary requirements, pillow preferences).
  • Browser & Client Data: Browser type, viewport resolution, and anonymized access logs for performance optimization.

2. How We Utilize Your Information

Collected guest information is processed strictly for legitimate hospitality operations:

  • Generating official booking reference codes and reservation vouchers.
  • Dispatching dining table confirmations and concierge responses within our 4-hour commitment.
  • Maintaining local browser preferences (such as recently viewed accommodations and wishlist items) via client-side storage.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We utilize browser localStorage to store your saved wishlist rooms and recently viewed room history. This information resides purely on your local device and is never shared with third-party tracking networks. No marketing tracking cookies are deployed.

4. Data Retention & Security Protections

Guest database records are stored in secure relational databases using encrypted access protocols, prepared SQL queries, and strict input sanitization. We do not sell, rent, or lease guest information to external advertisers or brokers.

5. Guest Inquiries & Data Rights

Guests have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their submitted contact records. To submit a data inquiry, please contact our administrative desk:
